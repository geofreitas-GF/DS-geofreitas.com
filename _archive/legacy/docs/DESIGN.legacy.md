## Layout

- **Radius:** 8px
- **Border weight:** 1px
- **Spacing:** 8px baseline grid (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64)

### Posture rules
- Editorial restraint: keep 35–40% of any viewport or slide as open whitespace, with clear typographic hierarchy and low visual chrome so content and metrics lead.
- Structural precision: align layouts to a 12-column grid with an 8px spacing scale (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64) and crisp 1px hairlines.
- Tonal elevation: separate sections with surface shifts (#ffffff → #f6f7f9 → #eceef2) rather than heavy drop shadows; shadows only for floating menus and dialogs.
- Contrast floor: body text at least 4.5:1 against its background; large text, icons and focus rings at least 3:1. Verified pairs: #161616 on #ffffff 18.6:1, #525252 on #ffffff 6.1:1, #ffffff on #303030 13.2:1, #161616 on #fefa54 16.8:1.
- Primary button: #303030 fill with #ffffff text, hover #4a4a4a, pressed #1f1f1f, 9999px capsule pill radius (shared across Primary, Default, and Dashed buttons, while text inputs remain 8px and status tags remain 4px); one primary button per view. Secondary button: white fill, 1px #e2e5ec border, #161616 text.
- Brand highlight: #fefa54 only for one marker, badge, underline or accent word per view, always paired with #161616 text or placed on a dark surface; never a light-mode button background and never light-mode text.
- Tags, pills and avatars: neutral tags (Primary, Info, live, beta, design, Most popular), avatars, the header label and the Info alert use #f5f5f5 with #161616 text and a 1px #e0e0e0 border. Status tags match the alert tints with #161616 text: success #defbe6 / #a7f0ba border, warning #fcf4d6 / #fddc69, error #fff1f1 / #ffb3b8. Never use a mid-grey such as #6e6e6e or #707070 as a light-mode fill.
- Links: #525252, darkening to #161616 on hover; never lighter on hover. Never rely on color alone for state; pair it with text, an icon or a shape change.
- Typography: Poppins for display and body. Headings 600/700 with 1.05–1.25 leading and slight negative tracking; body 400/500 at 16px minimum with 1.5–1.6 line height and 60–75 characters per line. Scale 12/14/16/18/22/26/32/38/48/64px.
- Plain-text copy fields: headlines, taglines and summaries never contain markdown syntax such as asterisks, leading dashes or hashes.
- Forms: labels #525252, input text #161616, 1px #e2e5ec borders; focus moves the border to #303030 with a visible 2px ring; errors use the word Error or an icon as well as color.
- Pitch decks: light slides use #ffffff or #f6f7f9 grounds with #161616 titles; accent slides use a #303030 ground with #ffffff titles and figures, one #fefa54 highlight at most, and a white call-to-action button with #303030 text. Slide headers, footers and page numbers stay at 4.5:1 or better.
- Dark mode: ground #141414, elevated #272727, borders #2a2f3c, text #dcdcdc; primary buttons invert to #f6f7f9 with #303030 text (12.3:1); #fefa54 may be used as highlight text on dark grounds.
- Motion: 150–300ms for small interactions, ease-out on enter and ease-in on exit; respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Rules

### Color and contrast
- Body text must reach at least 4.5:1 against its background; large text (16px+ or 14px bold), icons and focus indicators at least 3:1.
- Verified pairs: `#161616` on `#ffffff` 18.6:1 · `#525252` on `#ffffff` 6.1:1 · `#ffffff` on `#303030` 13.2:1 · `#161616` on `#fefa54` 16.8:1 · `#fefa54` on `#303030` 12.0:1.
- `#fefa54` is a highlight, never a wash. Use it for at most one element per viewport or slide: an active marker, a badge, an underline, or an accent word on a dark surface.
- Never use `#fefa54` as a light-mode button background or as text on a light surface (1.1:1 on white).
- Never rely on color alone for state; pair it with text, an icon or a shape change.
- Use only registered palette values. Functional colors (success, warning, error) come from the token files and are not brand colors.

### Typography
- Display and headings: Poppins 600/700, tight leading (1.1–1.25), letter-spacing −0.01 to −0.02em at 32px and above.
- Body and UI: Poppins 400/500, line height 1.5–1.6, line length 60–75 characters on desktop.
- Scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 26 / 32 / 38 / 48 / 64px. Body copy on screens is 16px minimum; 14px only for dense UI and metadata.
- Headlines are plain text. Never paste markdown syntax (`**`, `-`, `#`) into headline, tagline or summary fields.
- Load Poppins (400, 500, 600, 700) as a web font and keep the declared fallback stack so text renders if loading fails.

### Spacing and grid
- 8px base grid: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px; 4px only for icon-to-label gaps.
- 12-column grid, max content width 1120–1200px, gutters 24px (desktop) and 16px (mobile).
- Sections are separated by 64px+ and a tonal surface shift, not by shadows.

### Shape and elevation
- Corner radius 8px for controls and cards; 12px for large panels; full radius for pills and avatars only.
- Borders are 1px `#e2e5ec` (light) or `#2a2f3c` (dark).
- Shadows are reserved for floating layers (menus, dialogs) and stay soft and low-opacity.

### Components
- **Primary button:** `#303030` background, `#ffffff` text, 8px radius; hover `#4a4a4a`, pressed `#1f1f1f`. One primary button per view.
- **Secondary button:** `#ffffff` background, 1px `#e2e5ec` border, `#161616` text.
- **Text link:** `#525252`, darkening to `#161616` on hover; never lighter on hover.
- **Badge / highlight:** `#fefa54` background with `#161616` text, used sparingly.
- **Tags and pills:** neutral tags `#f5f5f5` fill, `#161616` text, 1px `#e0e0e0` border. Status tags reuse the alert tints with `#161616` text: success `#defbe6` / `#a7f0ba`, warning `#fcf4d6` / `#fddc69`, error `#fff1f1` / `#ffb3b8`. Never a mid-grey fill.
- **Avatars and label boxes:** `#f5f5f5` fill, `#161616` initials or text, 1px `#e0e0e0` border.
- **Info alert:** `#f5f5f5` fill with a 1px `#e0e0e0` border; success, warning and error alerts use the same tints as their tags.
- **Form fields:** 1px `#e2e5ec` border, `#161616` input text, labels in `#525252`; focus moves the border to `#303030` with a visible ring; errors use text plus color.
- **Focus:** every interactive element shows a visible focus ring of at least 3:1.

### Pitch deck and landing pages
- Light slides: `#ffffff` or `#f6f7f9` ground, `#161616` headlines, `#525252` supporting copy.
- Accent slides (cover, traction, ask): `#303030` ground, `#ffffff` headlines and figures; `#fefa54` only for one highlight word, kicker rule or marker.
- Calls to action on accent slides use a `#ffffff` button with `#303030` text.
- Keep slide headers, footers and page numbers at 4.5:1 or better; no pale grey chrome text.

### Dark mode
- Ground `#141414`, elevated surface `#272727`, borders `#2a2f3c`, text `#dcdcdc`.
- Primary buttons invert to `#f6f7f9` with `#303030` text (12.3:1) so they stay visible on dark grounds.
- `#fefa54` may be used for highlights and accent text on dark grounds (16.7:1 on `#141414`).

### Motion
- 150–300ms for small interactions, ease-out on enter, ease-in on exit; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.


## Component Accent & Motion Rules
- Letter avatars: #fefa54 Volt fill with #303030 Poppins SemiBold (600) initials, borderless 9999px circle.
- Text links & inline buttons: #161616 with a 1px #161616 animated underline (left-to-right on hover, right-to-left on exit).
- 3-tier radius system: 9999px capsule pills for interactive buttons, 8px for inputs/selects, 4px for metadata/status tags.

## Dark Mode Theme Rules
- **Surfaces & Text:** Deep neutral canvas (`#121212`), elevated card surface (`#1e1e1e` with `#2e2e2e` border), primary text (`#f5f5f5`), muted text (`#a3a3a3`).
- **Primary CTA Pill:** `#fefa54` Volt fill with `#161616` Charcoal text, inverting to `#ffffff` fill with `#161616` text on hover.
- **Links & Avatars:** Headings, table headers, and inline links use `#ffffff` pure white with a `1px` `#ffffff` animated underline; letter avatars retain `#fefa54` Volt fill with `#161616` SemiBold initials.
